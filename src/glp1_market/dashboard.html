Why are some early years blank?

Ozempic was approved for type 2 diabetes in 2017. Mounjaro was approved for type 2 diabetes in 2022. Wegovy and Zepbound were first approved for weight management in 2021 and 2023, respectively. Standard Medicare Part D excluded drugs when used for weight loss. In 2024, Wegovy gained a cardiovascular indication and Zepbound gained a sleep apnea indication, which made those uses eligible for Part D coverage.

The data show brand totals across covered uses. They don’t tell us why any particular cell is blank. CMS can hide very small counts, so blank values stay missing. FDA approval history · CMS coverage background · CMS coverage by indication

Canada · safety and market context

On November 25, 2026, Health Canada published a safety review that found a possible link between prescription semaglutide and NAION, a very rare but serious condition involving vision loss. The review focused on semaglutide, which includes Ozempic and Wegovy. Health Canada advisory

Obesity Canada cites IQVIA estimates of about 350,000 Canadian prescriptions for obesity medications in 2020 and over 3.4 million in 2024. These are national estimates for a broader category. Obesity Canada’s 2026 report

The study question is whether Canadian fills changed after the advisory. That takes comparable Canadian data before and after November 25, along with other events such as coverage or supply changes. U.S. Medicare fills cannot answer that question.

Forecast audit · synthetic data

Each model is fit using only months before its forecast. Last value carries forward the most recent observation.

WAPE is total absolute error divided by total observed volume or sales. Positive bias means overforecasting. Coverage is the share of held out observations inside each prediction interval. With few observations, coverage can swing sharply. The forecast chart displays the selected number of months ahead.
