Market overview

Share uses total prescriptions (TRx) across the four modeled brands. A prescription count can include refills and is distinct from a patient count.

Forecast audit

Each model is fit using only months before its forecast. Last value carries forward the most recent observation.

WAPE is total absolute error divided by total observed volume or sales. Positive bias means overforecasting. Coverage is the share of held out observations inside each prediction interval. With few observations, coverage can swing sharply. The 24 month view extends beyond the tested horizons.

Scenario explorer

Compare hypothetical business conditions with the statistical base forecast. Payer coverage means whether and under what conditions an insurer pays for a treatment.

The nutrition response is a simulation assumption. The gross figure applies synthetic revenue per prescription and excludes rebates and actual net pricing. Manufacturer HHI measures concentration within this four brand panel on a 0 to 10,000 scale.

What I’d check next

I’d use prescription and coverage data to check whether a change came from more demand or a shift between brands. For nutrition, I’d test the assumed lag with independent sales data before using it to plan inventory.
